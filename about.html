Hi, I am Daphne


A little about me and this space.

I completed my yoga teacher training in 2025 and have since begun sharing the practice with others through teaching.
Beyond teaching, I enjoy exploring yoga in its many forms — from the movements and the way we experience our bodies, to the philosophy that invites us to look a little deeper.

The more I explore, the more I realise there is always something new to learn, question, and discover.
So, I thought, why not create a space to share some of these explorations along the way?

Yoga offers me a space to observe — my body through movement, and my mind through the thoughts and reactions that arise.

It is an ongoing practice of getting to know myself a little better:
❀ Noticing my strengths and limitations
❀ Acknowledging what is there without immediately judging or criticising myself
❀ Recognising where there may be room to grow.

On and off the mat, we practise acceptance, humility, and kindness as ways of meeting ourselves and the world around us — with awareness, compassion, and understanding.
We are given the space to unlearn, relearn, and rediscover ourselves, slowly coming home to ourselves.

Now is the journey home.
