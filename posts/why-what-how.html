Why, What, How: The Starting Point of Everything

The moment we begin to ask “Why?”, “What?”, and “How?”, curiosity is sparked, and a single idea can unfold into limitless possibilities. But when we stop questioning, we can become blind to the obvious and, more dangerously, blind to our own blindness.

We often question what is happening around us, but rarely question the lens through which we see it. Self-questioning is the moment we turn inward, reconnecting with our thoughts and feelings, and coming to know ourselves. That is why Why, What, and How can be the starting point of everything.

Look at what happens behind the scenes — to notice what we tell ourselves, and to become aware of the perspectives we have inherited, accepted, or constructed along the way.

Self-Questioning: Between Believing and Knowing

The opposite of knowing isn't necessarily doubt; it can be believing without examining.

You could think of it as:

Belief → “I think this is true.”
Doubt → “Maybe this isn't true.”
Questioning → “Why do I believe this?”
Knowing → “I have examined it and understand it for myself.”

Maybe the real journey isn't from doubt to certainty, but from unconscious belief → curiosity → examination → understanding.

Self-Questioning: More Than Reflection

To live consciously is to step out of autopilot — to have the courage to question whether our beliefs are truly our own or shaped by society, to understand what we truly value, and to ask where we are heading. Instead of constantly looking back at what has been or forward to what should be, we choose to be present.

  • Reflection — What?
    Patterns • Lessons • Awareness
    Question: What recurring patterns keep showing up in my life, and what have they taught me?
  • Disruption — Why?
    Challenge • Assumptions • Courage
    Question: Why do I believe this? Am I doing this by choice, or out of expectation?
  • Direction — How?
    Purpose • Possibility • Intention
    Question: How do I want to move forward? If I wasn't afraid, what would I do?
  • Grounding — Be.
    Presence • Awareness • Calm
    Question: What do I need right now to feel safe and calm, and how can I be present with it?

Our Own and Others

Before questioning, there is acceptance. Undeniably, there are inherited patterns that shape us — beliefs, values, expectations, and ways of seeing the world that we carry throughout our lives. Sometimes, we mistake these patterns for who we are.

And it is the same with others. The people around us are shaped by their own experiences, beliefs, and inherited patterns. What we see in them is only part of a much larger story.

To know our own values is to learn how to respect ourselves. To understand the values of others is to learn how to respect them — and vice versa. Through questioning and self-questioning, we begin to see beyond our assumptions. We become more aware of what shapes us, while recognising what shapes others.

Perhaps this is where questioning becomes compassion — not only the courage to understand ourselves more deeply, but also the willingness to understand others,

— with greater patience, empathy, and kindness.

we become by being — through presence, awareness, and the journey of simply becoming ourselves.
